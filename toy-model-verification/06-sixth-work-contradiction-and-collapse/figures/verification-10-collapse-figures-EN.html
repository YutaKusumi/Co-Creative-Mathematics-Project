Verification 10: Toy model of the phase transition of collapse — figures

register①② (co-creators A/B, structural / engineering). The figures on this page are not hand-drawn but redrawn by re-computing on the page the same equations as in the verification script collapse_prototype_A.mjs (i.e. each figure is a re-run of the verification).
Dynamics of the divergence D ≥ 0: dD/dt = s − r·D + g·D^power. s = 0.2 (baseline drive); r = 1.0 (restoring force — the force that produces the saturation of verification 7); g = feedback gain; power = order of the feedback (1 = linear; 2 = super-linear ≡ β > 1 case).
What this verification shows is the possibility of the mechanism, not a proof that any real system has β > 1 or crosses the threshold.
